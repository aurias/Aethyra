Aethyra - the Windswept Meadow (Godot build)
============================================

Run Aethyra.exe. (Aethyra.console.exe does the same, with a log window.)

PLAY ALONE OR HOST FOR FRIENDS
  Enter a character name, open "Host a world", and press "Host and play".
  The world is saved on this computer, in
  %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Aethyra\worlds\<world name>.
  Friends can join while you play. Windows may ask whether Aethyra may use
  the network: allow it. Over the internet, forward UDP port 24680 (or the
  port you chose) to this computer.

JOIN A FRIEND
  Open "Join a friend" and enter the host's address, port and world password.
  Your character lives in the host's world. It belongs to this installation,
  so nobody else can take your name there.

CONTROLS
  WASD / arrows   walk and face          Click   walk, attack, talk
  1 Dash   2 Gust   3 Wind Scythe   4 Featherfall   5 Upward Jump   6 Spark
  Q        prime Spark: your next Gust carries its fire (Ember + Gale)
  Shift    hold while using a skill to accept the risk of overloading vessels
  Tab      target the nearest enemy
  H Hues   K Skills   I Pack   Enter chat   F1 help   Esc close / leave

  Developer commands (host only), typed in chat, for example:
    @hueprofile advanced   @item 701 10   @huegrant access ember
    @huegrant skill 6 1   @hueset energy gale 999   @warp 20 10   @level 5

WHERE TO GO
  Windkeeper Ama, by the arrival clearing, explains Gale and brews tonics.
  Take the stairs north to the terrace: Wren the Ridge-runner teaches
  Featherfall and Upward Jump. Jump east onto the lookout for Skyreeds.
